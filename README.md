## What is a stalled AI pilot?

A stalled AI pilot is a proof of concept that proved the model works and never reached production. The usual causes are KPIs nobody agreed up front, underestimated data integration, and no named owner for production. The fix is a scoped 90-day sprint tied to one financial KPI, preceded by a 1 to 2 day diagnostic that sizes the opportunity.

## What is agentic AI in MarTech?

Agentic AI in MarTech means AI agents that take actions across the marketing stack (segmentation, audience activation, journey orchestration, CAC and LTV optimisation) inside defined guardrails, with human approval on consequential steps. It runs on a composable CDP and clean identity resolution, so the data layer comes before the agent layer.

## Frequently asked

**Why do AI pilots fail to reach production?**
Usually because the business case and KPIs were never agreed up front, data integration was underestimated, and nobody owned the path to production. Model quality is rarely the blocker.

**How long does it take to move a stalled AI pilot to production?**
A scoped 90-day sprint, after a 1 to 2 day diagnostic that sizes the opportunity and agrees the KPI.

**What is a composable CDP?**
A customer data platform assembled from best-fit components on your own cloud warehouse (Snowflake, BigQuery, AWS), instead of one monolithic vendor suite. Identity, consent and activation stay under your control.

**Who do you work with?**
CHROs, CIOs, CDOs, CMOs and founders in BFSI, fintech and CPG who have a working proof of concept and no path to production.
